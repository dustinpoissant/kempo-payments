Payments

Every charge this site has taken, and the connection to the processor that takes them. Nothing here holds a card number — those go from the customer's browser straight to the processor, and what is kept is this site's record of what happened to the money.

Payments Disputes Take a payment Settings

A dispute (a chargeback) is a customer asking their bank to take a payment back. The bank holds the money while it decides, and you have until the due date to send your evidence. The evidence is sent from your Stripe account, where the form and the deadline emails already are; this tab shows what is open, how long is left and which sale each one is about, and takes you to the right one.
